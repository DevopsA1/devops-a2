Digital Resources & Community Facilities

Library services for learning, creating, and connecting.

Take the library with you through our online reading and research resources, or visit the branch to use study spaces, technology facilities, and community programmes.

Available Anywhere

Digital Library Services

Explore reading and research resources from home or on the go.

Read & Listen

E-Books & Audiobooks Portal

Borrow digital books and audiobooks from our online collection. Read on a computer, tablet, or phone, or listen while travelling and studying.

  • Open the library's digital reading portal.
  • Sign in with your active library card number.
  • Choose an available title and borrow it digitally.

Access: Requires an active library card number.

Study & Discover

Online Research Databases

Find reliable academic articles, reference materials, journals, and learning resources for assignments, projects, and personal research.

  • Visit the online research databases portal.
  • Enter your active library card number when prompted.
  • Search by subject, title, author, or keyword.

Access: Requires an active library card number.

News & Periodicals

Digital Newspapers & Magazines

Browse digital editions of newspapers and magazines to keep up with current affairs, culture, technology, and topics of interest.

  • Open the newspapers and magazines collection.
  • Sign in using your active library card number.
  • Select a publication and read its digital edition.

Access: Requires an active library card number.

Visit Your Local Branch

In-Branch Services

Facilities and activities are available during library opening hours.

Connect & Focus

Free High-Speed Wi-Fi & Study Rooms

Use free high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the branch. Quiet study rooms provide space for individual work, reading, and group study.

  • Connect to the library guest Wi-Fi when you arrive.
  • Ask library staff about study-room availability.
  • Follow the posted room-use and session timings.

Print & Prepare

Printing & Scanning Kiosks

Print study materials and scan documents using the self-service kiosks available inside the library.

  • Prepare your document on a supported device or storage medium.
  • Use the kiosk instructions to print or scan your pages.
  • Ask the service desk for help if you need assistance.

Learn & Participate

Community Workshops & Reading Clubs

Join library activities that encourage reading, creativity, digital skills, and knowledge sharing with other community members.

  • Check the library noticeboard for upcoming sessions.
  • Ask staff how to register for a workshop or reading club.
  • Attend at the date and time announced for the activity.

Getting Started

Your library card opens the digital collection.

Keep your active library card number ready when signing in to digital services. If you need a card or help accessing a resource, the library team can guide you.
