Frequently Asked

Questions from prospective members

Click any question to expand
What happens if I already own the seasonal hardcover pick?

Two weeks before each quarterly parcel ships, Patrons and Salon Fellows receive a private preview card and may exchange the primary volume for any alternate selection from our 148-book archive at no extra cost.

Are the reading circles fast-paced or deadline-heavy?

Never. Each selection is paced over four to six unhurried weeks—roughly forty pages between gatherings. If you fall behind, you are always welcome to join simply to listen over tea.

How are the digital e-books and printable folios delivered?

Digital books are provided in clean DRM-free EPUB format compatible with all e-ink tablets, paired with ink-light PDF margin trackers for Letter and A4 paper.

Can I gift a ReaderHaven membership to a friend?

Yes. Reach out through our correspondence desk on the Contact Us page and our librarians will prepare a physical wax-sealed welcome letter or scheduled digital invitation for your recipient.
